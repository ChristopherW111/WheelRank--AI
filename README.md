# WheelRank--AI
## WheelRank AI is an AI powered bike recommendation platform that helps riders find the best dirt bikes e bikes and pedal bikes based on their budget skill level and riding style.
## Target User
 Teen and young adult riders ages 13 to 20 who ride or want to buy dirt bikes e bikes BMX bikes and wheelie bikes and need help choosing the right bike.
## Problem
 Many riders have trouble choosing the right bike because there are so many options and reviews are spread across different websites. Buying the wrong bike can waste money and make riding less enjoyable.
# Why it matters
## WheelRank AI helps riders make better buying decisions by giving personalized recommendations instead of making them search through hundreds of reviews. This saves time money and helps riders find a bike that fits their goals.
## AI System Behavior
WheelRank AI uses Classification Detection and Reasoning Over Text and Images.
What the AI actually does in your system
The AI ranks bikes based on power reliability battery life price wheelie ability and upgrade potential. It can compare bikes identify bikes from pictures and recommend the best bike for each rider based on their answers.
##  Features
List and briefly explain the main features of your product
Personalized bike recommendations based on budget skill level and riding style
Bike comparison tool to compare two or more bikes
AI bike scanner that identifies bikes from pictures
WheelRank score that rates bikes for wheelies reliability value and performance
AI upgrade recommendations for riders who want better performance
Save favorite bikes and compare them later
AI review summaries that combine thousands of reviews into easy ratings
